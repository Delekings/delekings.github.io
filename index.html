About

Bridging Technology,
Ethics & Policy

I am a lawyer, AI ethicist, and policy advocate with over five years of experience shaping how artificial intelligence is governed — particularly across Africa and the Global South. My work sits at the crossroads of law, technology, and social justice.

As the founder of the Pan-Africa Center for AI Ethics, I built one of the continent's most active AI ethics communities from the ground up — reaching researchers, policymakers, and practitioners across 36 countries through training, research, and advocacy.

I co-drafted Nigeria's first National AI Policy, contributed to the NIST Generative AI Public Work Group, and have engaged with the UN, UNESCO, and the African Union on AI governance frameworks. My research focuses on data justice, algorithmic transparency, and what inclusive AI development truly looks like in practice.
